Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

What CityScope is

CityScope is an individual student portfolio project built and operated by Stephan Wasalathanthrige. It is not a registered company. This policy explains, in plain terms, what happens to information when you use CityScope.

What information is collected

CityScope does not have user accounts, does not use cookies, and does not run analytics or advertising tracking.

The information involved when you use the service is:

  • City search terms. When you search for a city, the search information is sent from your browser to the CityScope backend. The backend uses it to look up weather, air quality, and currency data.
  • Location coordinates, only if you choose "Use my location". See the "Location access" section below for exactly what is sent and what isn't.
  • Platform request logs. The backend is hosted on Azure App Service. Azure's configured HTTP access logging records the requesting IP address and the full request path for requests received by the backend. The request path can contain the city name searched for. These logs are retained for up to 3 days on the hosting platform and are automatically removed according to the configured retention policy. CityScope's application code does not read, export, or analyze these logs, and they are not sent to an analytics or advertising service.

What is stored only on your device

Your recent searches, pinned ("favorite") cities, theme choice, and temperature unit preference are saved using your browser's local storage.

This data:

  • Never leaves your device or gets sent to CityScope's backend
  • Is not accessible to Stephan Wasalathanthrige through CityScope
  • Can be cleared at any time using the "Clear" button next to Recent Searches, or by clearing your browser's site data for the page

Location access

CityScope can request your device's location, but only when you explicitly choose "Use my location" in the app. It is never requested automatically on page load, and the feature is entirely optional — you can search for a city by name instead.

When you click "Use my location":

  • CityScope first shows its own explanation, before your browser's native location permission prompt appears.
  • If you allow it, your browser reports your device's location (latitude and longitude — depending on your device and browser, this can be precise, not just approximate).
  • Those coordinates, together with your browser's timezone, are sent to the CityScope backend for that one request — the same backend, and the same kind of request, used for an ordinary city search — and are used only to fetch weather, air quality, and currency data for that location.

What CityScope does not do with your location:

  • It is not reverse geocoded into a city or country name. CityScope has no reverse-geocoding capability; the dashboard shows "My Location" rather than a guessed city or country.
  • It is not sent to any third-party geocoding or mapping provider.
  • It is not written to your browser's local storage.
  • It is not added to Recent Searches or My Cities (Favorites).
  • It is not retained by CityScope's backend beyond handling that one request, other than the same short-lived cache and platform request log described elsewhere in this policy, on the same terms as any other search.

You can deny or revoke location access at any time through your browser's site settings for CityScope. If access is already blocked, CityScope shows a message explaining how to re-enable it rather than prompting you again.

Third-party services used

CityScope uses the following third-party services to provide its features. These services operate independently and are subject to their own policies and practices:

  • Open-Meteo — used by the CityScope backend for geocoding, weather, and air quality data. Your browser does not contact Open-Meteo directly.
  • exchangerate-api.com / open.er-api.com — used by the CityScope backend for currency exchange rates. Your browser does not contact these services directly.
  • Esri (ArcGIS Online) map tiles — when you open the Map view, your browser requests map tiles directly from Esri's infrastructure. These requests may include your IP address and are subject to Esri's own policies. Required Esri attribution is displayed on the map.
  • unpkg.com — your browser loads the Leaflet mapping library directly from this CDN. The CDN may receive standard technical request information associated with serving that file.
  • Microsoft Azure — the frontend is hosted on Azure Static Web Apps and the backend is hosted on Azure App Service. The backend hosting environment retains the short-lived HTTP request logs described above. No analytics or tracking service is attached to the Static Web App.

CityScope does not collect your name, email address, account information, or payment information.

Cookies

CityScope does not set or use cookies — including after adding the "Use my location" feature described above. Location access is a browser permission, not a cookie, and CityScope does not store a separate "location consent" preference of any kind. There is no cookie-consent banner because there are no cookies to consent to.

The site uses browser local storage for the preferences and locally stored information described above (recent searches, favorites, theme, and unit preference). Location coordinates are never written to local storage. None of this local storage is sent to the CityScope backend.

Data retention

CityScope's backend does not maintain a permanent database of your searches.

The backend uses a short-lived in-memory cache to avoid unnecessary repeat requests to upstream weather, air-quality, geocoding, and currency providers. This cache clears automatically when the service restarts and is subject to the cache periods described in the Terms of Use.

Separately, Azure App Service's configured HTTP request log is retained for up to 3 days before being automatically removed according to the configured retention policy.

Recent searches, favorites, theme settings, and temperature-unit preferences stored in your browser remain on your device until you clear them.

Your choices

You can use CityScope without creating an account.

You can clear locally stored data at any time from within the app or through your browser settings. You can also stop using the service at any time.

Because Azure's short-lived hosting logs are generated by the hosting platform, stopping use of CityScope does not immediately remove a request that has already been recorded in those logs. Those logs are configured to be retained for no more than 3 days.
