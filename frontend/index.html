              About CityScope

              A fast, beautiful way to check any city's weather and essentials.

              What this is

              CityScope answers one question: "What is happening in this city right now?" Search any city and instantly see its current weather, hourly and 7-day forecast, air quality, local time, and currency, all in one place. No sign-up, no account, no paywall.

              Good to know

              • Everything you see is live data. Nothing is made up or estimated.
              • Your recent searches and pinned cities are saved only on this device, in your browser's local storage. They are never sent to any server.
              • Weather and air quality come from Open-Meteo; currency rates from exchangerate-api.com. See the Privacy Policy for details.
