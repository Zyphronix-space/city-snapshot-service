              About CityScope

              A fast, beautiful way to check any city's weather and essentials.

              What this is

              CityScope answers one question: "What is happening in this city right now?" Search any city and instantly see its current weather, hourly and 7-day forecast, air quality, local time, and currency, all in one place. No sign-up, no account, no paywall.
