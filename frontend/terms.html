What CityScope is

CityScope is a free, no-account tool that lets you search a city and see its current weather, hourly and 7-day forecast, air quality, local time, and currency exchange rate, sourced live from third-party APIs (Open-Meteo for weather/air quality/geocoding, and open.er-api.com / exchangerate-api.com for currency). See the Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.

No warranty, "as is"

CityScope is provided "as is," with no guarantee of accuracy, availability, or uptime. In particular:

  • Weather, air quality, and currency data come from free third-party APIs that CityScope does not control, and that data may itself be delayed, estimated, or occasionally wrong.
  • To reduce load on those free APIs, CityScope caches results for a short time before re-fetching (up to 24 hours for city lookups, down to 5 minutes for weather, 10 minutes for air quality, and 30 minutes for currency rates) — figures shown may not reflect the very latest upstream update.
  • If an upstream provider is unavailable, the affected part of the dashboard (for example, air quality or currency) may show as unavailable rather than failing the whole page.
  • The "Travel Snapshot" shown on the dashboard is a simple, rule-based summary of numbers already shown elsewhere on the page (temperature, rain chance, UV, air quality) — it is not AI-generated, not professional travel or safety advice, and should not be relied on for safety-critical decisions.

Acceptable use

CityScope's backend shares a single rate limit across all visitors to protect the free upstream APIs it depends on (see the source code for specifics). Please don't attempt to automate high-volume requests, scrape the API for bulk data collection, or otherwise use the service in a way that could exhaust that shared quota for other visitors.

No account, nothing to lose

CityScope has no accounts, no payments, and no server-side storage of your activity beyond a short-lived cache described in the Privacy Policy. You can stop using it at any time with nothing left behind on any CityScope server.
